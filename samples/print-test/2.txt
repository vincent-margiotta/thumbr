The back of a Luhmann card was rarely used.
When it was, it usually held a continuation
the author could not cut, or a cross-reference
added years later.

The front is the argument. The back is the
residue: what would not compress, what arrived
too late, what belongs here and nowhere else.
---back---
A card with a back is not two cards. It is
one card that refused to finish on the front.

Whether that is a virtue or a failure depends
on whether the second side adds a new claim or
merely restates the first at greater length.
