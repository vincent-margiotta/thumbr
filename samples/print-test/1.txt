The zettelkasten is not a filing system. It is a
writing machine. The cards are not stored thoughts
but active hypotheses — each one waiting to be
contradicted or confirmed by the next card it touches.

A card is useful not because of what it contains but
because of where it sits. Its address in the sequence
is its meaning. Move it and you move the idea.

Luhmann's mistake was never making a single card. He
made the system and the system made him. By the end
he could not write a sentence without consulting the
box first.

The box does not think for you. It thinks with you.
That is the difference between a tool and a method.
Most tools extend the hand. This one extends the thought.
