This card intentionally exceeds the maximum
card face size and will be refused by the
print script.

It demonstrates that the size limit is enforced
consistently between the in-app editor and the
print utility.

An oversized card contains either a single idea
that has not yet been compressed, or multiple
ideas that have not yet been separated.

In either case the remedy is the same: cut.
Find where this card becomes two cards and make
the split. The address space is unlimited.
There is no penalty for creating more cards.

The only penalty is for keeping ideas together
that belong apart.

(This card should be split before printing.)
