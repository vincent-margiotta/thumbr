Compression is the primary quality test.

If an idea cannot fit on one card face it is
not yet a single idea. It may be two ideas, or
an idea and its justification, or an argument
with unstated premises.

The discipline is to find the cut: where does
this idea end and the next one begin? The cut
is the intellectual work. Writing the card is
the confirmation that the cut was clean.

Cards that resist compression are drafts.
Cards that compress cleanly are notes.

Luhmann on compression: a card that runs long
is evidence that the thinking is not finished.
The card is not the thought; it is proof that
the thought was completed.
