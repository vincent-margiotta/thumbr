Every book contains perhaps three ideas worth
arguing with. The rest is context, evidence,
and elaboration that becomes noise once the
core claim is extracted.

Reading to extract those three ideas means
reading differently: not to understand the
whole, but to locate the claims that interrupt
your current thinking most sharply.

The three ideas are the ones that generate
immediate objections or unexpected connections
to cards already in the box.
